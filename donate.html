Support Magnolia Regional

Gifts from our community help us add equipment, expand spaces for patients and families, and keep care close to home. Every gift, of any size, makes a difference.

This is a concept site built for a class project, so it does not process real donations or payments. Each option below sends a request to our team, who would follow up with secure giving instructions. Send a question about giving any time.

One-time gift

A flexible gift you make once, on your own schedule, for whatever amount feels right to you.

How it works

  • Supports the area of greatest need, unless you tell us otherwise
  • Common gifts range from about $25 to $500, though every amount helps
  • A written acknowledgment is provided for your records
  • Takes just a few minutes to get started

Getting started: Send a request and our giving team will follow up with secure next steps.

Monthly giving

Steady, smaller gifts given every month add up to reliable support we can plan programs around.

How it works

  • Spreads your support into a smaller monthly amount
  • Change or cancel anytime by contacting us
  • Counts toward our monthly giving circle
  • Helps fund ongoing programs, not just one-time needs

Getting started: Send a request and our giving team will follow up with secure next steps.

Memorial and tribute gifts

Honor someone, a loved one, a caregiver, a nurse or a doctor, by giving in their name.

How it works

  • Give in memory of someone you have lost
  • Give in honor of a caregiver who made a difference
  • We can send a card to the family or honoree, if you would like
  • Share any names or messages in your request

Getting started: Send a request and our giving team will follow up with secure next steps.

Corporate and matching gifts

Many employers match charitable donations, which can double or triple the value of a gift.

How it works

  • Ask your employer whether they match charitable gifts
  • We can provide documentation for matching gift programs
  • Sponsorship opportunities are available for classes and community events
  • Our team can walk you through the process

Getting started: Send a request and our giving team will follow up with secure next steps.

Planned giving

Leave a lasting legacy for Magnolia Regional through your estate or financial plans.

How it works

  • Include Magnolia Regional in a will or living trust
  • Name us as a beneficiary of a retirement account or life insurance policy
  • Speak with your own financial or legal advisor first
  • Our team can share sample language if it would help

Getting started: Send a request and our giving team will follow up with secure next steps.

Volunteer your time

Giving does not have to be financial. Volunteers are some of our most valuable supporters.

How it works

  • Greet visitors and help families find their way
  • Support waiting areas and patient comfort programs
  • Orientation sessions run throughout the year
  • No experience needed, just a few hours a month

Getting started: Send a request and our volunteer services team will follow up with next steps.

Where your gift goes

Community giving supports the parts of care that go beyond what insurance and billing cover on their own.

Emergency and trauma care

Faster equipment upgrades and expanded capacity when every minute counts.

Family support spaces

Comfortable waiting areas and rooms for families staying overnight with a loved one.

Technology and equipment

Updated imaging, monitoring and surgical equipment across our departments.

Community health programs

Free screenings, classes and outreach for the community beyond our walls.
