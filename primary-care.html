Primary care

Check-ups, screenings and an ongoing relationship with one care team that gets to know you. Choose a visit type to see what it covers and how to get started.

No referral needed for primary care. Not sure what you need? Send us a request and we will get you with the right clinician.

Annual physicals and wellness visits

A yearly check-up to review your health, update your history and catch small concerns before they grow.

What a visit usually covers

  • Weight, blood pressure and basic vitals
  • A review of your health history and medications
  • Preventive screenings due for your age
  • Time to ask questions about anything on your mind

Getting started: No referral needed. Most adults schedule this once a year.

Vaccinations and preventive screenings

Routine vaccines and screening tests, timed to your age and risk factors, to catch problems early or prevent them altogether.

What a visit usually covers

  • Flu, shingles, tetanus and other routine vaccines
  • Cholesterol and blood sugar screening
  • Cancer screenings due for your age
  • A plan for what is due next and when

Getting started: No referral needed. Many screenings can be added onto an annual physical.

Chronic conditions

Ongoing support for conditions like diabetes, high blood pressure or high cholesterol, with one team tracking your numbers over time.

What a visit usually covers

  • Diabetes and blood sugar management
  • Blood pressure monitoring and medication review
  • Cholesterol and heart-health follow-up
  • Lifestyle guidance that fits your routine

Getting started: No referral needed. Bring a list of current medications to your first visit.

Referrals and care coordination

When you need a specialist, your primary care team makes the referral and keeps your full picture in view, from lab results to specialist notes.

What a visit usually covers

  • A referral to the right specialist for your concern
  • Lab and imaging orders when needed
  • Notes shared between your care teams
  • A single place to ask questions about your overall care

Getting started: Start with your primary care team. They will coordinate the rest.
